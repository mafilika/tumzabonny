About Tumza Bonny

Tumza Bonny (Pty) Ltd is a B-BBEE Level 1 company registered with CIPC in 2019. We take part in property development and construction, supply government and private clients with hardware, stationery and consumables, and deliver plumbing, painting, roofing and maintenance services to schools, clinics, correctional services, municipalities and resorts. Our vision is to become a competitive, capable company generating sustainable income and jobs.
